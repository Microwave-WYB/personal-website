## Yibo Wei

I am a PhD student at the University of California San Diego (UCSD), with broad interests in IoT, automotive, and cybersecurity research. I am interested in understanding real-world security problems and developing practical ways to address them.

## Research interests

My interests are broad and evolving across systems, security, and computing. My work so far includes IoT, automotive systems, cybersecurity, and studies of online scams, but I am not limited to these areas.
